# RESURRECT 2026 idea submission

## Project
FloodGuard AI — AI-powered, location-aware flood-risk insights and emergency decision support.

## Team
Shalini, Ritheeksha, Shree Madhura, Sushmitha

## Core concept
Combine documented rainfall, terrain, land-cover and historical flood information to estimate an area-level risk category, explain contributing factors and display preparedness guidance.

## Prototype boundary
A geographically limited demonstration using documented data and a validated baseline. Simulated data, if used for the demo, will be labelled clearly and will not be represented as live observations.

## Responsible-use statement
Not an official warning system. Users must follow local authorities and emergency services.
